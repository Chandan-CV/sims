# SIMS

SIMS is a photo search app for your phone. Instead of scrolling through
your camera roll, just type what you're looking for — "beach sunset",
"dog on a couch", "birthday cake" — and SIMS finds it.

Everything runs entirely on your device. Your photos are never uploaded
anywhere.

## How it works

1. **Index your photos** — the first time you open SIMS, it scans your
   photo library and builds a searchable index. This can run in the
   background, so you can keep using your phone while it works.
2. **Search with plain language** — describe what's in the photo, not
   file names or dates.
3. **Browse results** — tap into any photo, share it, open it in your
   gallery app, or save it to favorites.

Once indexed, new photos you take are picked up and added automatically.
